# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Techies @ VITM is committed to providing a respectful and welcoming environment for students and contributors.

Everyone participating in the community is expected to contribute to an environment where people can learn, collaborate, ask questions, and share ideas comfortably.

## Expected Behaviour

We encourage:

* Respectful communication
* Constructive discussions
* Helpful feedback
* Collaboration
* Openness to different perspectives
* Giving credit to others' work
* Responsible sharing of information

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

* Harassment or bullying
* Hate speech or discrimination
* Personal attacks
* Threats or intimidation
* Deliberate disruption
* Sharing someone's private information without permission
* Impersonation
* Malicious or deceptive behaviour
* Unwelcome sexual or graphic content
* Repeated unwanted contact or targeting

## Handling Problems

If you encounter behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the Techies @ VITM moderation team through the community's designated reporting channel or support system.

Reports will be handled as responsibly and privately as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to contributions, discussions, Issues, Pull Requests, and other interactions associated with the Techies @ VITM project.

## Enforcement

Moderators and maintainers may take appropriate action when behaviour violates this Code of Conduct.

Possible actions may include:

* Warning
* Removal of inappropriate content
* Temporary restriction
* Removal from the project or community

Serious violations may result in immediate action.

**Learn. Build. Collaborate.**
