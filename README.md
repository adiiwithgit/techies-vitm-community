# 💻 Techies @ VITM

> **One community for everything tech at VIT Mumbai.**

**Techies @ VITM** is a student-led technology community connecting students across VIT Mumbai around technology, innovation, projects, opportunities, open source, hackathons, and collaboration.

## 🌐 Community

The community brings together:

* 💻 Technology enthusiasts
* 🏛️ Tech clubs and student communities
* 🚀 Hackathon participants
* 🛠️ Developers and builders
* 🌎 Open-source contributors
* 💡 Students working on projects and ideas
* 🤝 Students looking for teammates and collaborators

## 🎯 What We Do

### 💬 Community

A place for students to discuss technology, share knowledge, ask questions, and connect with others.

### 🚀 Opportunities

Discover hackathons, workshops, internships, competitions, open-source opportunities, and other technology-related opportunities.

### 🛠️ Projects

Collaborate on student projects and community initiatives.

### 🌎 Open Source

Learn Git and GitHub, contribute to open-source projects, and help other students get started with open source.

### 🏛️ Tech Communities

Discover and connect with technology-focused clubs and communities at VIT Mumbai.

## 🤝 Contributing

Everyone is welcome to contribute.

You can contribute by:

* Improving documentation
* Adding useful resources
* Suggesting community initiatives
* Fixing errors
* Creating useful tools
* Contributing to community projects
* Helping other students get started with GitHub and open source

Before contributing, please read [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Techies @ VITM aims to be:

* Open to students from every branch
* Independent from individual clubs and organizations
* Respectful and inclusive
* Focused on learning and collaboration
* Community-driven
* Open to experimentation and innovation
