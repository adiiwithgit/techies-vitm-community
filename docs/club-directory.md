# 🌐 CAMPUS-WIDE TECH COMMUNITIES

These communities operate across branches and are not limited to a single department.

### GDG on Campus

**Google Developer Groups on Campus**

Focus areas may include development, Google technologies, open source, cloud, AI, and related technologies.

### CSI

**Computer Society of India**

A professional computing community with activities around technology, computing, learning, and student development.

**AWS Builder Groups**
